## Turn the idea into *a test.*

::: {.row .c2}
::: {.card}
::: {.lab}
The option
:::

A weekly first-draft pack for the bid team, with a named reviewer and a source
check on every figure.
:::

::: {.card .hi}
::: {.lab .a}
The test
:::

Run it on four live bids. Measure hours saved, rework rate, and one quality
score from the reviewer. Decide after four, not one.
:::
:::

::: {.foot}
**Kill rule:** if rework does not fall by the second bid, stop.
:::

::: notes
This is the slide to argue with. The kill rule is what makes it a strategy
rather than a pilot that never ends.
:::

## Write the brief *the tool cannot fill in.*

```text
Task:     first-draft response to a client brief
Context:  the brief, our last three responses, the reviewer's rules
Format:   section headings, 800 words, no invented citations
Check:    every figure traced to a source; reviewer signs off
```

::: notes
Show the four lines. The check line is the one people leave out. That is the one
that makes it usable.
:::
