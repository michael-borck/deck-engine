# Portrait Deck

One idea per screen. Built for phones, Shorts and a side panel.

::: notes
This deck is portrait by default. The canvas is 720 × 1280, the chapter rail sits
at the bottom, and every slide inherits the orientation from the front matter.
:::

---

::: {.k}
Why portrait
:::

## The same engine. *A different canvas.*

::: {.card .st reveal=1}
::: {.lab}
Portrait
:::

720 × 1280. The rail moves to the bottom and the type re-fits to the narrower
column.
:::

::: {.card .hi .st reveal=2}
::: {.lab .a}
Mixed
:::

A single slide can override the deck default with `orientation=landscape`.
:::

::: notes
Show that nothing in the engine is hard-coded to landscape. The stage resizes,
the rail moves, and the rehearsal notes and presenter keep working.
:::

The test

## One slide. *One idea.*

> If it needs two ideas, it is two slides. Portrait is unforgiving that way.

::: {.foot}
**Use portrait** for a phone handout, a Shorts clip or a side panel. Keep dense
tables for landscape.
:::

::: notes
Ask the room: which of their slides would survive a phone screen? That is the
portrait test.
:::
